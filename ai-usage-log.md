## AI-Assisted Development Approach

AI was used as a development assistant rather than as a replacement for the project development process.

The development workflow involved:

1. Defining the required feature or problem.
2. Using AI to explore implementation approaches.
3. Generating or modifying code with AI assistance.
4. Running and testing the implementation locally.
5. Identifying errors and debugging them with AI assistance.
6. Iterating on the implementation.
7. Deploying and testing the final application.

The final project was tested and deployed by the developer.
